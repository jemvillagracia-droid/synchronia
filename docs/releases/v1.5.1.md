Download **SYNCHRONIA-Setup-1.5.1.exe** (installer) or **SYNCHRONIA-1.5.1-portable.zip** (no install) below. Upgrading keeps your data.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose *More info › Run anyway*.

### Fixed
- **Timer sound:** Windows could keep the audio engine suspended until you clicked, so the alarm stayed silent. Now:
  - the app may play sound without a click;
  - the audio engine is unlocked on your first click or key press;
  - every sound waits until audio is running.
- **New timer sounds:**
  - pressing **Start** plays a short "engage" call, so you hear straight away that sound works;
  - a **Test sound** button sits on the timer page, and tells you if this computer cannot play sound.
- **Full-screen timer:** the timer fills the whole work area, with no empty space below. The display, digits and controls scale with the window. On small windows the help line and sync monitor fold away so the time stays large.
- **Quieter sub-page switches:** the page transition plays only when you move to another area. Sub-pages and tabs of the same area (for example the Professional Portfolio sections) switch instantly.
- **Back to top:** every page and tab change now starts at the top of the page.
- **Research Development:** *Capture* is now **New project**. Type a working title to create the project and open it, or press it with no title for the full form.
