Download **SYNCHRONIA-Setup-1.3.0.exe** (installer) or **SYNCHRONIA-1.3.0-portable.zip** (no install) below. Upgrading keeps your data.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose *More info › Run anyway*.

### New: import your school calendar
On **Academic Calendar**, choose **Import school calendar** and pick the academic calendar your school released (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, or paste its text).

**What it reads**
- Single days.
- Ranges, e.g. "Oct. 19–24" and "Dec 21, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027".
- Lists, e.g. "March 15, 16 and 17".
- Tables, and calendars laid out under month headings ("AUGUST 2026" then "12 Start of classes").
- Dates without a year, which are placed in the school year ("SY 2026–2027").

**How each date is filed**
- As classes, examinations, holiday, break, enrolment, deadline or school event.
- Days without classes are marked and shown striped on the calendar.

**Saving and undoing**
- Before saving you see every date with the line it came from, and can edit or untick any of them. Dates worth a second look are marked, for example an ambiguous 03/04.
- After saving, the calendar opens where the school year begins.
- Importing the same calendar again does not add duplicates, even for dates you renamed.
- Each import can be removed in one step. Its dates go to the recycle bin.

Pictures or photos of a calendar cannot be read; paste the text instead.

- After a month change, the calendar could briefly show another month's items. It now always shows the month you are on, and the day panel follows the month.
