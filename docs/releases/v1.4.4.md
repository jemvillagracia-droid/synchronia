### 2nd quality check
This release completes the system-wide audit in the "SYNCHRONIA 2nd quality check" directive. The full report is in [`docs/QA_REPORT_2.md`](https://github.com/jemvillagracia-droid/synchronia/blob/main/docs/QA_REPORT_2.md).

**Fixed**
- **Form errors in record pop-ups:** a form with errors now shows a message under every field that needs attention, plus a summary ("Not saved — check the 2 fields marked below. Everything you entered is kept."). Before, only a brief browser tooltip appeared, on the first field only.
- **"SYNC" readout:** the Central Nexus "SYNC … THIS WEEK" readout is now labelled "ACTIVITY". It counts records changed this week; SYNCHRONIA works on this computer only and nothing is synced to a cloud.

**Verified by new tests**
- **Typing in pop-ups:** Shift + letters and Shift + numbers type capitals and symbols. Accented and non-English characters, Backspace and Delete work. Tab stays inside the pop-up, Enter never closes it, and Escape closes only the top one.
- **Saving:** a failed save never shows a success message, and the pop-up keeps what you typed.
- **Navigation:** back, forward and reload work, and closing with unsaved changes asks first.
- **Search, filter, sort and the recycle bin** all work.
- **Crash recovery:** autosaved work survives the app being killed, the database passes its integrity check, and the app restarts normally.
- **Backup and restore** return the data to the backup's moment.
- **Repeat exports:** exporting the same CV twice gives identical content.
- **Small screens:** no sideways scrolling at tablet and phone widths.
- **Performance:** with 10,000 records, lists, search and the calendar answer in a few milliseconds, and the Central Nexus in under 0.1 s.
