Download **SYNCHRONIA-Setup-1.2.0.exe** (installer) or **SYNCHRONIA-1.2.0-portable.zip** (no install) below.

**Install:** run the installer. It installs per user (no administrator rights). Upgrading from 1.1.0 keeps your data; a snapshot of your database is taken before the update, and older syllabi are turned into editable documents the first time you open them.

**Portable:** unzip `SYNCHRONIA-1.2.0-portable.zip` to a folder you can write to (not Program Files) and start `SYNCHRONIA.exe`. Your records stay in the `SYNCHRONIA Data` folder beside the program.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose *More info › Run anyway*.

### New
- **Research Development Workspace**: projects by stage with milestone-based progress and suggested next steps; literature review (duplicate check, APA 7, RIS/BibTeX, matrix export); instruments from first draft to final locked version; ethics, submissions, funding and collaborators; manuscripts kept as links to your Word, PDF or Drive files.
- **Syllabus as a document**: open a syllabus and edit it like a Word document, with tables, pictures and GIFs, cut/copy/paste, autosave and recovery snapshots; export to Word or PDF.
- **Command Center**: calendar quick entry (click a day on the Central Nexus), the **System Timer** (full-screen countdown, warnings, system-down alert and recharge breaks) and the **Idea Moodboard** (an endless board of ideas for each project, course or syllabus, filled from Quick Capture).
- **Themes**: purple (default), light, sepia and seven neon colour combinations.
- **Paper size** in Settings › Document export: Short/Letter, Long/Folio, A4, Legal, A5 and B5 for every export.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** throughout (press **?** for the list), including zoom (Ctrl + / − / 0) and F11.
- SOUL ARCHIVE-style grouped sidebar, with module tabs listed on the left.

### Fixed
- Pressing Shift no longer closes pop-ups.
- PDF exports now use the chosen paper size. Before, they always printed on Letter.
- Hovered buttons keep readable text.
- Settings no longer fill in signatories or vision/mission statements for you.
