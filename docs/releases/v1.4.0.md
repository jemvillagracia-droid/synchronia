### New: PDS Section VII — L&D interventions attended, by year
In **Professional Portfolio › Professional Development**, choose **PDS · L&D attended by year**.

**The yearly list**
- Your trainings and seminars appear year by year in the Personal Data Sheet (CS Form 212) layout:
  - title;
  - inclusive dates, written dd/mm/yyyy;
  - number of hours;
  - Type of L&D (Managerial, Supervisory, Technical, Foundation);
  - conducted / sponsored by.
- Each year shows its total hours.
- Sort by date, **newest first** (as the PDS asks) or **oldest first**. The exports follow the order you choose.

**Filling it in**
- Set the Type of L&D right in the table, or for every training that lacks one in a single click. Rows missing hours, a sponsor or a full date are pointed out.
- Talks you gave are left out, since the PDS lists what you attended.

**Folders, export and import**
- Link each year to its **Google Drive folder** of certificates and open it from that year.
- Export a year, or all years, to **Excel** (with the Section VII headings), **Word** or **PDF**, on your chosen paper size.
- Import the L&D rows from your existing PDS (Excel), checked before saving. Rows already in your portfolio are skipped.

### Improved
- **System Timer** sits in the centre of the page.
- **CV Studio**
  - A header card shows the name, position and contact details the CV prints, with a link to fix anything missing.
  - Empty sections are tucked away behind one switch, and the tabs are numbered.
  - The editor works in smaller windows. Before, the editing panel collapsed to a thin strip below about 1100 px.
- **Record pop-ups** can be opened from a link (used by the new automated test that types into every pop-up).

### Fixed
- PDF tables now keep the column widths set in Word or in the editor. Before, every column in a PDF came out equal.
- Small contrast fix in the Sepia theme.
