Download **SYNCHRONIA-Setup-1.5.0.exe** (installer) or **SYNCHRONIA-1.5.0-portable.zip** (no install) below. Upgrading keeps your data.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose *More info › Run anyway*.

### SYNCHRONIA is now one faculty system
- **Administrative Operations removed:** correspondence, document routing, secretariat records, the office log, turnover documents and the administrative reports are gone from the app. Nothing was deleted: those records stay in your database, your backups and the portable export. Their tasks moved to *General*. Meetings stay as calendar entries.
- **No more modes:** the Administrative / Instructor / Researcher / Faculty Development switcher is gone. The Central Nexus always shows the faculty layout.
- **No duplicate sidebar entries:** the sidebar lists main compartments only. Literature Review, Extension, Faculty Development, Professional Timeline and Backup and Recovery open from their own compartments.

### New: Credentials Tracker (Professional Development)
Built on DBM-CHED Joint Circular No. 01, s. 2026 (Enhanced Implementing Guidelines of JC No. 3, s. 2022), Annex A.
- **All four KRAs:** 83 requirements with their points, criterion maximums and required documents.
- **Suggested claims:** your portfolio records are matched to their requirement. Claim them one by one, or all at once.
- **Grid per criterion:** like a spreadsheet tracker. Under each requirement, your claimed records are rows and the required documents are checkbox columns: tick for secured. Right-click a box (or press P, M or N) for pending, missing or not applicable. The *Documents* tab lists every document still to do.
- **Planned goals:** degrees in progress, exams and books are kept apart. Their points appear as "when completed".
- **Import your Excel tracker:** education, licences, creative works, resource speakerships, research, organisations and awards come in as records, claims and document statuses.
- **Export to Excel, Word or PDF:**
  - Excel has a Summary sheet, one sheet per KRA with ✔ / ✘ / … / — per document, and *Documents to secure* and *Planned goals* sheets.
  - Word and PDF are landscape, with the same grids; every file is re-opened and checked before it is reported saved.
- **Estimates only:** points are estimated from your own claims. The evaluation committees decide what is credited.

### CV Studio
- **New default CV type: DBM-CHED reclassification CV.** Its sections follow the four KRAs in order, with KRA headings.
- **Analyzer check:** every KRA must be represented, within Annex K's five-page limit.
- **Administrative CV:** no longer offered for new CVs. Existing ones still open and export.

### System Timer
- **Battle march:** "Operation Sortie", an original battle march made for SYNCHRONIA (brass, timpani, snare, strings). It loops seamlessly at "system down" until you recharge, restart, silence or dismiss, and stops on its own after 5 minutes. A short brass call sounds at the warnings.
- **Sound settings:** Settings › Notifications chooses the battle march or simple beeps, sets the volume, and has a preview.
- **Animated display:** hex field, sweeping scan line, rotating target rings, scrolling hazard band, a 50-segment power bar, a three-trace sync monitor, and red alarm states. It runs at a steady 60 fps.
- **Fixed:** the top-bar chip now shows "DOWN" legibly.

### Smoother everywhere
- **Faster start:** pages load separately and preload when the app is idle. The first screen downloads about a quarter of before (309 KB instead of 1.4 MB).
- **Page switches:** measured at 50–80 ms.
- **System-style motion:** a scan line sweeps each page as it opens, page titles decode from system glyphs, pop-up windows open from a centre line like a monitor, and buttons show a light sweep on hover. Frame times were measured on every change: a steady 60 fps. *Reduce motion* turns all of it off.
