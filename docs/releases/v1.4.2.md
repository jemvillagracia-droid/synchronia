### Export quality pass
Every CV template, and every other export, was rendered page by page and inspected. Word files were also opened in a second program (LibreOffice) to confirm they match the PDFs.

**Curriculum vitae**
- **Contact line:** it now breaks between items. A long web address (e.g. an ORCID link) is never split mid-number, and no "|" is left hanging at the end of a line.
- **Date column:** the Traditional and Comprehensive templates have a wider date column, so "Jun 2016 – May 2020" and "Aug 2023 – Present" stay on one line. A licence's "Valid until …" moves under the licence name.
- **Word headings:** section headings now print in capitals in Word, as they do in the PDF.

**Research documents**
- **Table columns:** every table is sized to its content. Instrument items get the full width instead of a narrow middle column, and the expert-review headings no longer break mid-word.

**PDS — L&D attended**
- **PDF layout:** dates (dd/mm/yyyy), hours and Type of L&D fit their columns.
- **Small paper:** on A5 and B5 the six columns print in landscape.
- **Excel:** rows are sized to their wrapped text.

**All PDFs**
- **Narrow columns:** text too long for a narrow table column wraps inside it instead of running into the next column.
