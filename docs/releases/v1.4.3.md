### L&D list in the SYNCHRONIA document style
The PDS "Learning and Development Interventions Attended" export now looks like every other SYNCHRONIA document.

**Word and PDF**
- **Font:** Times New Roman, matching the reports, minutes and turnover documents.
- **Top of the page:**
  - a centred title, with the subtitle "Personal Data Sheet (CS Form 212), Section VII" and the year;
  - your name, position and institution from your profile;
  - a one-line summary (number of interventions, total hours, date format).
- **The table:** then the Section VII table, in the PDS column order.

**Excel**
- **Same font:** Times New Roman. The PDS layout is kept, so rows can still be copied into the official form.

**Research PDFs**
- **Matching font:** the progress report, literature matrix, instrument, ethics and publication record PDFs now use Times New Roman, like their Word versions (they printed in Arial before).
- **Syllabus:** keeps Arial, following the university syllabus format.
