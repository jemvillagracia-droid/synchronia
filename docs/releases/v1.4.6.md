Download **SYNCHRONIA-Setup-1.4.6.exe** (installer) or **SYNCHRONIA-1.4.6-portable.zip** (no install) below. Upgrading keeps your data.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose *More info › Run anyway*.

### Fixed
- **Number tiles no longer split words:** labels such as "PRESENTATIONS" in the Central Nexus (and other pages' number tiles) could break mid-word ("PRESENTATION / S"). Tiles now stay wide enough for their longest word. This was checked at five window widths.
- **Plain wording:** the Central Nexus and the Professional Profile no longer mention "Phase 3", a leftover from development.

Everything from [1.4.5](https://github.com/jemvillagracia-droid/synchronia/releases/tag/v1.4.5) is included: calendar photos, the backup copy folder and notification settings.
